1. Introduction

CASA Governance ("we," "us," "our") provides an API for AI governance, risk assessment, and policy enforcement based on NIST frameworks. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use our API ("Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described herein.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Account and Authentication Data: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and company name. You authenticate via an API key (X-CASA-API-Key). We log API key usage for billing and security purposes.
  • Request Payloads: To perform governance checks, audits, knowledge queries, or synthetic content risk assessments, you send us data such as:
    • Descriptions of AI actions, system architectures, use cases, and risk tiers.
    • Metadata for traceability.
    • Content type, provenance flags, and distribution context for synthetic content analysis.
    • Natural language queries about AI governance.
    We process this data solely to provide the requested analysis and generate a response.
  • Technical Logs: We automatically log IP addresses, timestamps, request parameters, and error codes for debugging, security monitoring, and service improvement. These logs are kept for a limited period.
  • Cookies (Web Dashboard only): Our web dashboard uses essential session cookies. We do not use tracking cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your requests and manage your account.
  • Execute governance checks, audits, knowledge queries, and synthetic content assessments.
  • Generate traceability records (trace IDs) for audit replay.
  • Improve and maintain Service performance (anonymized aggregate usage patterns).
  • Comply with legal obligations or enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use your request payloads to train machine learning models, build profiles, or for any purpose beyond delivering the Service.
