## Mapping rule

Framework labels in the registry identify control families only. They do not assert that one canonical control by itself fully satisfies every clause, Annex control, criterion, point of focus, or implementation requirement in that family. External-assurance preparation must expand this crosswalk to the exact licensed/current requirement identifiers and record applicability, implementation, evidence and exceptions.
